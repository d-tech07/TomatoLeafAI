    Diseases & treatment

    How it works

    1. Photo check — blur, exposure and resolution.
    2. Background removal — the leaf is cut out; soil, stones, shadows and weeds are removed while diseased spots are kept.
    3. Is it a tomato leaf? — leaf structure (shape, serrated edge, veins) plus a deep check against the tomato training data. Anything else is rejected with an explanation.
    4. Diagnosis — the proposed LAG-HViT (lesion-attention-guided hybrid CNN–Vision Transformer) or any baseline model; confidence is temperature-calibrated. Below {{ '%.0f'|format(conf_threshold * 100) }}% the app says it is not sure.
    5. Explanation — Grad-CAM shows where the model looked; the Leaf-Focus Score says how much of that attention is on the leaf, and disease regions are highlighted.
    6. Treatment — management advice for the predicted disease.
